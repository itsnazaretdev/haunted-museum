## Life

Microsoft launched the MSN Messenger service in July 1999. It let people chat in real time with
their friends from their computer, and it became a huge part of being a teenager in the 2000s.

People customized everything: nicknames with symbols, display pictures, personal messages and
the song they were listening to. And the nudge, which made your friend's chat window shake, was
the best way to get attention. At the end of 2005 it became Windows Live Messenger.

## Death

Social networks and smartphones changed how people talked. On January 11, 2013, Microsoft
announced it would retire Messenger and replace it with Skype. In April 2013 users were moved
to Skype, and the little green and blue friend went offline.

## Defense

For millions of people, it was their first social network. Friendships, crushes and endless
after-school conversations happened there, long before WhatsApp existed.

## Why it should come back

It argues that a chat where you could "appear offline", without read receipts or "last seen",
gave people more peace than any app today. And it points out that even Skype, the one that
replaced it, is gone now too.
