## Life

Internet Explorer arrived in 1995, in the early days of Windows 95. It grew fast during the
"browser wars" against Netscape Navigator and, by the early 2000s, it was used by the vast
majority of people on the internet.

Internet Explorer 6 was released in 2001. For many years it was simply *the* web for millions
of people, in offices, schools and public libraries.

It also gave the web some important things: it pushed the idea of making requests to the
server without reloading the page, the technique that later became known as AJAX.

## Death

Firefox and then Google Chrome took its users away. Developers grew tired of fixing layouts
only for Internet Explorer. Microsoft launched Edge as its replacement, and on June 15, 2022
the desktop Internet Explorer 11 app was officially retired on most versions of Windows 10.

## Defense

It made the web reach normal people. It was the first browser for a whole generation, and part
of the technology behind modern dynamic web apps started inside it.

## Why it should come back

It shouldn't, really, and it half knows it. But it argues that the web needs to remember what
happens when one company controls the browser everyone uses.
