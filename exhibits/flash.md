## Life

Flash started in 1996 as a small animation tool called FutureSplash Animator. Macromedia bought
it and turned it into Macromedia Flash, and in 2005 Adobe acquired Macromedia.

During the 2000s Flash was everywhere: browser games, animated websites, music players, intros,
cartoons and the first popular online video players. A huge number of people learned to make
games and animations thanks to it.

## Death

In 2010 Steve Jobs published an open letter called "Thoughts on Flash" explaining why the
iPhone and iPad would not support it. Mobile browsers moved to HTML5, CSS animations and
JavaScript instead. Adobe announced in 2017 that Flash would end, and support officially
finished on December 31, 2020. Early in 2021, Flash content stopped running in the Flash Player.

## Defense

Flash gave creativity to a whole generation. Before HTML5 could do any of it, Flash already had
animation, sound, video and games in the browser. Projects like Ruffle and the Flashpoint
archive exist today because people did not want its games to disappear.

## Why it should come back

Not as a plugin, but as an idea: a simple tool where anyone could draw, animate and publish
something interactive in one place.
