## Life

Nintendo released the Game Boy in Japan on 21 April 1989. It was invented by Gunpei Yokoi,
who had already created the Game & Watch handhelds. Unlike those, the Game Boy could play many
different games on cartridges.

Nintendo included Tetris with the console, and the two became almost the same thing in
people's minds. Its rivals had color screens, but they used much more power, so their batteries
lasted far less. Including its later versions, the Game Boy family sold over 100 million units
in the 1990s.

The family kept growing: Game Boy Pocket (1996), Game Boy Color (1998), Game Boy Advance (2001)
and Game Boy Micro (2005), the last new model.

## Death

From 2004 the Nintendo DS started to replace the Game Boy line, and after the Game Boy Micro
in 2005 there were no more new Game Boys.

## Defense

It proved that a simple, cheap and tough console with great games could beat more powerful
machines. Many kids played their first video game on it.

## Why it should come back

It argues that a game you can play for hours with two AA batteries, no updates, no internet and
no ads is the purest kind of fun.
