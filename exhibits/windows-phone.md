## Life

Windows Phone 7 launched in 2010 with a completely new design language: flat, typographic and
based on Live Tiles, squares on the home screen that updated with live information. Many people
thought it looked fresh compared to the grids of icons on other phones.

In 2011 Microsoft partnered with Nokia, and Nokia Lumia phones became the face of Windows Phone.
In 2014 Microsoft bought Nokia's phone business.

## Death

Users did not arrive, and without users, developers did not build apps. Without apps, users
did not arrive. Microsoft moved to Windows 10 Mobile, but it never recovered. Support for
Windows 10 Mobile ended in December 2019.

## Defense

Its flat design influenced the look of many interfaces that came later. It was fast even on
cheap phones, and its camera and design were loved by the people who used it.

## Why it should come back

It argues that two phone platforms are not enough, and that the world would be better with a
third option that thinks differently.
