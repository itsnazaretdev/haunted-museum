## Life

Clippy, officially called Clippit, appeared in Microsoft Office 97 in 1997 as the default
character of the Office Assistant. Its job was to watch what you were doing and offer help.
Its most famous line was: "It looks like you're writing a letter. Would you like help?"

Before Clippy, Microsoft had tried a cartoon-style interface called Microsoft Bob in 1995, which
was a big flop.

## Death

Many people found Clippy intrusive: it appeared without being asked and interrupted their work.
In 2001 it was turned off by default, and Bill Gates even presented its "retirement" at a public
event. In 2007 it was removed completely.

## Defense

It was one of the first attempts to give software a friendly assistant that tries to understand
what you are doing. Years later, people missed it so much that in 2021 Microsoft brought Clippy
back as an emoji in Microsoft 365 after a tweet asking for it got huge support.

## Why it should come back

It argues that it was simply too early: today everyone has an assistant, and it was the first.
