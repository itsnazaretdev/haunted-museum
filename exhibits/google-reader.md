## Life

Google Reader launched in 2005. It was a feed reader: you subscribed to blogs and websites
through RSS or Atom, and all their new posts appeared in one place, in order, without an
algorithm deciding what you could see.

It was loved by journalists, bloggers and curious people. It also had its own small social
features for sharing articles with friends.

## Death

In March 2013 Google announced it would close Reader, saying its usage had declined. It shut
down on July 1, 2013. Many users were very upset, and the shutdown is still remembered as an
example of how a free service can disappear even when people depend on it.

## Defense

It protected an open idea of the web: you choose what to follow, and you get everything in
order. No ads in the middle, no algorithm, no account on every website.

## Why it should come back

It argues that RSS never died, only Google Reader did, and that people tired of algorithms
should take control of their own feeds again with open tools.
