## Life

Sony launched the Walkman in 1979. It was small enough to carry in your pocket and play your own
cassettes with headphones, anywhere. For the first time, people could walk down the street with
their own soundtrack.

It became a symbol of the 80s. People recorded mixtapes for friends and crushes, and learned to
rewind a cassette with a pencil to save batteries. Sony sold more than 200 million cassette
Walkmans.

## Death

CDs, MiniDiscs and then MP3 players and phones replaced the cassette. In 2010 Sony announced it
had stopped making and selling the cassette Walkman in Japan.

## Defense

The Walkman invented personal, portable music. Every music app and pair of wireless earbuds
today follows the idea it started in 1979.

## Why it should come back

It argues that a mixtape made by hand, song by song, says more than any playlist an algorithm
can generate.
