## Life

Vine was founded in 2012 and bought by Twitter before it even launched. The app was released in
January 2013. Its rule was simple: videos of only six seconds that played in a loop.

That limit made people incredibly creative. Vine created a new kind of short comedy and many
of its creators became famous. Lots of internet jokes and memes from that time were born there.

## Death

Instagram added video, and later other short-video apps appeared. Vine's most popular creators
moved to platforms where they could earn money. In October 2016 Twitter announced it would shut
Vine down, and in January 2017 it became a read-only archive.

## Defense

Vine proved that very short, looping, vertical video could be a whole culture. Many of the
formats that short-video apps use today were tested on Vine first.

## Why it should come back

It argues the internet is too long now, and that six seconds is the perfect amount of everything.
